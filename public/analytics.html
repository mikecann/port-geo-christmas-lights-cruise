Site analytics

We use PostHog to understand how visitors find the competition, view and share entries, sign in, enter and vote. This helps us improve the website.

Analytics records the type of action, public entry and competition identifiers, and broad traffic sources such as social links or QR codes. It does not include your name, email, street address, form answers, full page URLs or Google sign-in details.

PostHog uses the connection IP address to estimate country, region and city, then discards the IP address. We remove coordinates, postcode and other detailed location fields before events are stored. City estimates can be inaccurate, especially with mobile networks or VPNs. We do not request your device's location.

We do not use session recordings, automatic click capture or analytics person profiles. A random identifier is kept in this tab's session storage so we can connect steps during a visit, including a sign-in redirect. It expires on a subsequent page load after 30 minutes and is not a permanent account identifier.

Analytics data is processed by PostHog in the United States. We respect your browser's Do Not Track signal. Analytics blockers may also prevent collection, and the website continues to work when analytics is unavailable.

This page describes site analytics. Information you deliberately submit to enter the competition or vote is handled separately as part of those services.
